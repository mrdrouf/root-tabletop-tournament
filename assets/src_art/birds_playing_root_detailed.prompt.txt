SUBJECT — the four Eyrie Dynasties leaders from the board game Root, all of them birds (see the
attached leader-card sheet for exactly how they look):
  * a hunched VULTURE in a brown robe, wings folded, deeply unimpressed
  * a brown HORNED OWL, wings folded, glaring straight ahead
  * a white-headed BALD EAGLE in a blue-grey robe, chin up, above it all
  * a WOODPECKER with a white-and-black head and an orange crest

They are sitting around a round wooden table PLAYING THE BOARD GAME ROOT. This is the single most
important thing: the second attached image is the Root map board, and it must be open on the table
between them as the centrepiece — the autumn woodland map with its clearings, paths and forests —
with little wooden playing pieces and a few cards on and around it. They are playing a BOARD GAME.
They are NOT playing cards and NOT playing poker.

All four wear flat, deadpan, utterly serious expressions, as if the game has been going for six hours
and nobody is enjoying it.

MOOD AND COMPOSITION — borrowed from C. M. Coolidge's "Dogs Playing Poker" paintings: a single low
warm lamp hanging over the table, a dark smoky room falling away into shadow around it, tumblers of
amber drink, lit cigars with curls of smoke, one bird leaning back, one leaning in over the board.
Take the lighting, the framing and the comic solemnity from those paintings — but the game on the
table is Root, not poker.

STYLE — match the attached reference art exactly. This is Kyle Ferrin's Root artwork: heavy black ink
contour lines, flat muted gouache colour, visible pen hatching, warm autumnal palette, charming
storybook animal characters with large simple eyes. Absolutely no photorealism, no 3D rendering, no
airbrushed gradients, no digital painting gloss.

Square aspect ratio, high detail.
