Design a SCHEMATIC EMBLEM -- a flat, minimal logo mark. NOT an illustration, NOT a scene. Think a
stamped brass club badge or a woodcut printer's device: it must still work embossed on a coin 32

THE MARK — a CIRCULAR EMBLEM, PERFECTLY SYMMETRICAL about its vertical centre line:
  * FOUR BIRD HEADS in flat silhouette, ALL THE SAME SIZE, arranged in a shallow arc across the upper
    half of the circle -- the outer pair set slightly lower than the inner pair and mirroring each
    other exactly across the centre line. EVENLY SPACED, with a CLEAR GAP of at least half a head's
    width between neighbours. NO HEAD MAY TOUCH OR OVERLAP ANOTHER; each is its own separate solid
    shape with clean empty space around it. They are told apart by OUTLINE ALONE, so exaggerate:
      - far left, a VULTURE: bald domed head, heavy deeply hooked beak, ruff at the neck
      - inner left, a HORNED OWL: round head, two sharp upright EAR TUFTS, short curved beak
      - inner right, a BALD EAGLE: smooth head, long strongly curved beak, heavy brow
      - far right, a WOODPECKER: long straight dagger beak, tall spiky swept-back CREST
    Give each head exactly ONE eye, as a single small notch of empty space. No pupils, no catchlights.
  * BELOW THEM, one SINGLE STRAIGHT HORIZONTAL BAR spanning the circle: the table edge. A plain bar
    and nothing else -- NO LEGS, no perspective, no table top.
  * SITTING ON THAT BAR, dead centre, a small ABSTRACTED ROOT MAP: a plain flat hexagon holding five
    dots joined by straight thin lines, and one simple wavy line for a river. Diagrammatic, like a
    circuit or a metro map.
  * A clean concentric DOUBLE RING border, both rings of even weight, perfectly circular.
  * NO LETTERING, NO NUMBERS, NO TEXT ANYWHERE AT ALL. Type is added separately.

HOW IT MUST BE DRAWN — this is the whole brief:
  * ABSOLUTELY FLAT. Every shape ONE single uniform colour with hard clean edges. No gradients, no
    shading, no highlights, no texture, no grain, no distressing, no weathering, no brush marks, no
    speckles, NO stray or scattered pixels anywhere.
  * EXACTLY THREE COLOURS, no more: a deep near-black brown for the birds and the table bar, a burnt
    orange for the rings and the hexagon, and empty TRANSPARENT background everywhere else.
  * BOLD AND SIMPLE. Thick even weights, generous spacing. If a detail would disappear at 32 pixels,
    leave it out entirely.
  * The background is fully TRANSPARENT -- no cream, no colour, no scene, no room, no lamp, no depth.
    Just the emblem floating on nothing, centred, with an even margin all round.
