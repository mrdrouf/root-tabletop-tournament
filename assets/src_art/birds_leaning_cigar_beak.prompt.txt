1. MORE NATURAL BIRDS. The current ones are stiff mascots -- each a near-perfect symmetrical oval with
   a badge-like face planted dead centre. Draw them as real birds instead, observed rather than
   designed:
   * TRUE ANATOMY. A proper bird shoulder where the wing joins the body, wings that fold in believable
     layers with primaries crossing at the back, a real neck, chest and brow. Feathers in natural
     MASSES -- throat, breast, shoulder, wing coverts -- not decorative stripes.
   * ASYMMETRY. Nothing mirrored. Each head turned or tilted a little off centre, one shoulder higher
     than the other, weight settled onto one wing. Bodies are organic and slightly irregular, not
     ovals.
   * SPECIES CHARACTER, from life: the VULTURE round-shouldered with a scrawny bare neck and a
     hunched, heavy-beaked stoop; the HORNED OWL broad and soft-edged with a flat facial disc and its
     tufts at an angle; the BALD EAGLE lean and hard-eyed with a sharp brow and a smooth white head;
     the WOODPECKER slim and upright with a long straight bill and a stiff crest.
   * Keep the expressions DEADPAN and completely serious, but make the gaze natural rather than
     cartoon-angry -- half-lidded, tired, unimpressed, each looking somewhere slightly different.

   glint, and a darker rim on the shadowed side. Flat shapes, but unmistakably shiny -- not a dull
   disc. It should be the brightest thing in the picture.
