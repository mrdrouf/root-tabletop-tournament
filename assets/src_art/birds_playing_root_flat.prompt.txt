THE SINGLE MOST IMPORTANT REQUIREMENT — THIS ARTWORK WILL BE SHOWN AS A SMALL ICON, ABOUT 128 PIXELS
WIDE. It must stay readable when shrunk that far. So: BOLD, FLAT, SIMPLE, GRAPHIC. Few large shapes,
strong silhouettes, high contrast, a limited palette. Think a screen-printed poster or a woodcut, not
an illustration.

Explicitly avoid: cross-hatching, stippling, fine pen texture, wood grain, dense foliage, engraving
lines, tiny background objects, rendered shading, gradients, soft focus, photorealism, 3D. The
previous attempt failed because it was packed with fine detail that turned into grey mush when
scaled down. Strip the detail out. Leave big clean areas of flat colour.

SUBJECT — four bird leaders from the board game Root, sitting around a round table PLAYING THE BOARD
GAME ROOT (the attached sheet shows the four characters):
  * a hunched VULTURE, bare orange head, brown robe, wings folded
  * a brown HORNED OWL with big yellow eyes and ear tufts, wings folded
  * a BALD EAGLE, white head, yellow beak, blue-grey robe
  * a WOODPECKER, black-and-white head, bright orange crest
All four are birds. All four wear flat, deadpan, completely serious expressions.

THE BOARD MUST READ CLEARLY. It is the centrepiece on the table, drawn large and SIMPLIFIED: a few
big pale clearing circles joined by thick clear paths, a winding blue river, and forests suggested by
a handful of large flat green shapes — NOT hundreds of little trees. A viewer should instantly see
"a board game map" as a graphic shape. Put a few chunky wooden pieces on the clearings in orange and
blue.

MOOD — a smoky back-room game night, in the spirit of the "Dogs Playing Poker" paintings: one warm
lamp low over the table, everything beyond it falling to near-black, a couple of simple amber
tumblers, one lit cigar with a single clean curl of smoke. Keep the background almost empty and very
dark — a flat dark shape, no furniture, no pictures on the wall, no clutter.

STYLE — Kyle Ferrin's Root character design: charming storybook birds, big simple eyes, chunky
shapes, warm autumnal palette of orange, cream, teal and deep brown. Bold black outlines of even
weight. Flat colour fills with no shading inside them.
