cream, teal, ochre and deep brown, and characters full of dry comic personality. It must feel
hand-made, warm and quirky — like a page from an illustrated folk tale.

It must NOT look like flat vector art, clip art, or a simple cartoon with uniform outlines. Keep the
painterly texture and the wobble of a real drawn line.

forest built from hundreds of tiny trees, no clutter of little props. Big confident shapes, strong
