THE POSTURE — they are HUNCHED OVER A BAR. Not standing, not perched: LEANING on it, the way four
regulars lean on a bar counter late at night.
  * A horizontal BAR TOP runs along the very BOTTOM EDGE of the image. All four are behind it, leaning
    forward onto it.
  * Their WINGS ARE FOLDED AND RESTING ON THAT BAR TOP -- forearms laid along it, taking their weight,
    right at the bottom edge of the image.
  * SHOULDERS HUNCHED UP around their necks, heads pushed forward and DOWN, chins low, so each bird
    is a compact rounded hunch rather than an upright figure. Slouched, settled in, elbows out.
  * Only their upper bodies show; everything below the bar is hidden behind it. No feet, no legs.
  * They lean at slightly different angles -- one further forward on both elbows, one propped on one
    wing, one with shoulders squarer -- so the row is not four identical poses.

HOW TO DRAW IT:
  * ABSOLUTELY FLAT. Every shape one uniform colour, hard clean edges. No gradients, no shading, no
    highlights, no texture, no grain, no speckles, no stray pixels.
  * EXACTLY FOUR COLOURS: deep near-black brown, warm parchment cream, burnt orange, muted dark teal.
  * BOLD AND SIMPLE, low detail, strong silhouettes, thick even weights. Readable very small.
  * DO NOT DRAW THE BAR ITSELF and do not draw a checkerboard: the background must be genuinely
    EMPTY AND TRANSPARENT. No counter, no ledge, no ground, no shadow, no scene, no frame, NO TEXT.
    Only the four birds, with their folded wings ending flush at the bottom edge of the image.
  * Leave no margin at the bottom -- the resting wings touch the bottom edge. Small even margin at
    the top and sides.
