WHAT TO DRAW — FOUR BIRDS PERCHED IN A ROW, and nothing else:
  * All four stand on the SAME invisible horizontal line at the very BOTTOM of the image, with their
    FEET VISIBLE and gripping that line, toes curled over a ledge. The bottom of the image is where
    the ledge is: their feet must sit ON that bottom edge, not float above it.
  * EVENLY SPACED across the width, all roughly the same height, facing the viewer. A CLEAR GAP
    between neighbours -- NO BIRD MAY TOUCH OR OVERLAP ANOTHER. Each is a separate solid shape.
  * Left to right:
      - a VULTURE: bald orange domed head, heavy hooked beak, pale neck ruff, a lit cigar in his beak
      - a HORNED OWL: two sharp upright ear tufts, big round spectacles, holding a small open book
      - a BALD EAGLE: white head, orange hooked beak, a peaked military cap, holding a small tumbler

  * All four DEADPAN and completely serious, looking straight out at the viewer.
  * They are plump, chunky, simplified little birds -- rounded bodies, short legs, big simple eyes.

HOW TO DRAW IT:
  * ABSOLUTELY FLAT. Every shape one uniform colour with hard clean edges. No gradients, no shading,
    no highlights, no texture, no grain, no speckles, no stray pixels.
  * EXACTLY FOUR COLOURS: a deep near-black brown, a warm parchment cream, a burnt orange, and a
    muted dark teal. Nothing else.
  * BOLD AND SIMPLE, low detail, strong silhouettes, thick even weights. Readable very small.
  * The BACKGROUND IS FULLY TRANSPARENT. No sign, no ledge, no perch drawn, no ground, no shadow, no
    scene, no circle, no frame, NO TEXT OR LETTERING ANYWHERE. Only the four birds on nothing.
  * Leave no empty margin at the bottom -- the feet touch the bottom edge of the image. Leave a small
    even margin at the top and the sides.
