The things that are not obvious

Root: Tournament Edition

The numpad hover a piece, press

0
Send home Warrior, wood: supply bag. Building, token: rightmost empty slot of its kind. Locked piece, card, hireling: nothing.
1
Take a warrior One of your warriors to the pointer. From the warriors' numpad 4 row first; none there: from the supply.
2
Take a token or building Hold 1 s on a token or building: that is now your kind. Tap: one piece of your kind to the pointer. From its numpad 4 row first; none there: from its supply.
3
Prisoner Warrior lies flat, locked, black outline. Press again: stands up.
4
Set a home row Select pieces, hold 1 s: their spots become the home row of that kind. Numpad 0 sends a piece of that kind to a free spot of the row; row full: to the supply. Numpad 1 takes a warrior from the warriors' row; numpad 2 takes its chosen kind from that kind's row. Numpad 4 again on a kind: the old row of that kind is forgotten.
no
pad
No numpad Options → Game Keys: "Move back to supply", "Move a warrior", "Move any token", "Set warrior as a knave prisoner", "Set home of selected pieces".

The box score beside the map

  • Reads the VP markers on the track. Rows follow the seats.
  • A turn pass writes the finishing faction's score. A point scored before your turn shows at once.
  • Marker on a dominance card: turn and suit recorded, DOM WIN offered. Vagabond, Knaves: COALITION.
  • END TURN: records the highlighted faction. For tables without the TTS turn order.
  • EDIT: cell = score, column number = round, portrait = whose turn, ▲ = order, ▼ = leader, captains, character. MAP DECK UNPICKED, game name.
  • EXPORT: JSON to the Notebook tab "BoxScore". Opens the Root Database token box.
  • INFO: the manual.

The VP panel above each Crafted Improvements board

  • – +: marker one step.
  • DRAW 1 CARD: to your hand. DRAW POND: from the pond.
  • Owner only.

The turn panel next to the box score

  • ROUND: from the box score. Clock: the current turn.
  • START: turn 1. Shuffles the deck, clears the sheet. Asks if the sheet holds a game.
  • DEAL 5 CARDS: to every seat. Asks before a second deal.
  • PAUSE / CONTINUE.
  • Red flash after 20 min.

The draft

  • Random seats and turn order. One selector board per seat. First click takes the faction.
  • Your faction: your last pick. Numpad 1, 2 and the VP panel follow it.
  • Knaves: 4 captains dealt, keep 3. A captain card in a slot spawns its meeple, items, warrior.

The deck holder

  • Card on the discard: face up.
  • Dominance card: its slot. Frog card: the pond. Shuffle-on-discard card: the deck.
  • Card on the pond: face up. Draw pile: never touched.

Resync setup board, bottom row

  • Re-sends every object to every player. For missing pieces, card backs.
  • Refused while anyone holds a piece with the mouse. Cards in hand: fine.
  • Removes a duplicated loose card.

The setup board

  • A wipe button turns red and asks. Press again within 3 s.
  • New game: keeps the map. Map button: keeps the factions. Marsh: rebuilt when the player count changes.
  • Box score, turn panel: never destroyed.
  • MORE: RIVERBOAT (Flotilla hireling), CREDITS.
  • FACTION CARDS: vagabond characters too.
  • Suit-marker snaps: enclaves only.
