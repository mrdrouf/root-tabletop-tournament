The things that are not obvious

Root: Tournament Edition

The numpad hover a piece and press

0
Send it home Warriors and wood go back into their supply bag, buildings and tokens into the rightmost empty slot of their kind. Locked pieces, cards and hirelings are left alone.
1
Take a warrior One of your warriors appears at your pointer: from the row set with numpad 4 first, otherwise from your supply.
2
Take a token or building Hold one second on a piece to choose its kind. Each tap then brings one of that kind to your pointer.
3
Knave prisoner Lays the hovered warrior flat, locked, outlined in black. Press again to stand it up.
4
Set the home Select pieces and hold one second: their spots become the home row of that kind. Numpad 0 fills it one per spot, then the supply; numpad 1 and 2 take from it first. A new numpad 4 on a kind replaces its row.
no
pad
No numpad Bind the five keys in Options → Game Keys. They start with "Move back to supply", "Move a warrior", "Move any token", "Set warrior as a knave prisoner", "Set home of selected pieces".

The box score beside the map

  • Reads the VP markers on the track. Rows follow the seats. Each turn pass writes the finishing faction's score; a point scored before your turn shows at once.
  • A marker settled on a dominance card records turn and suit and offers DOM WIN. A Vagabond or the Knaves get COALITION instead.
  • END TURN records the highlighted faction when the TTS turn order is off.
  • EDIT: click a cell to type a score, a column number to set the round, a portrait for whose turn it is, ▲ to reorder, ▼ for leader, captains or character, plus MAP DECK UNPICKED and the game name.
  • EXPORT writes the game to the Notebook tab "BoxScore" and opens a box for the Root Database token.
  • INFO is the manual.

The VP panel above each Crafted Improvements board

  • – + move that faction's marker one step.
  • DRAW 1 CARD deals one to your hand. DRAW POND draws from the frog pond.
  • Only the faction's owner can use it.

The turn panel next to the box score

  • ROUND comes from the box score. The clock is the turn in progress.
  • START: turn 1, shuffles the deck, clears the sheet. Asks first when the sheet holds a game.
  • DEAL 5 CARDS to every seat. Asks before a second deal.
  • PAUSE / CONTINUE stop and resume the clock.
  • The panel flashes red after twenty minutes.

The draft

  • Seats and turn order are random. Each seat gets a selector board; the first click takes that faction.
  • Your faction is the one you picked last. Numpad 1, numpad 2 and the VP panel follow it.
  • Knaves: four captains dealt, keep three. A captain card in a board slot spawns its meeple, items and warrior.

The deck holder

  • A card dropped on the discard turns face up.
  • A dominance card goes to its slot, a frog card to the pond, shuffle-on-discard cards into the deck.
  • A card dropped on the pond turns face up. The draw pile is never touched.

Resync setup board, bottom row

  • Re-sends every object to every player. Use it when someone is missing pieces or sees card backs.
  • Does not run while anyone holds a piece with the mouse. Cards in the hand are fine.
  • Afterwards a duplicated loose card is removed.

The setup board

  • A button that wipes something turns red and asks. Press again within three seconds.
  • A new game leaves the map; a map button leaves the factions. The Marsh is rebuilt when the player count changes.
  • The box score and the turn panel are never destroyed.
  • MORE: RIVERBOAT (Flotilla hireling) and CREDITS.
  • FACTION CARDS also lays out the vagabond characters.
  • The snap points beside the suit markers take enclaves only.
