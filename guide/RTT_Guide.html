The things that are not obvious

Root: Tournament Edition · the mod's hidden helpers, on two pages

The numpad always on, nothing to spawn: hover a piece and press a key

0
Send it home Any piece goes back where it belongs: a warrior or wood into its supply bag, a building or token into the rightmost empty slot of its kind, a tunnel to its own spot. Works on anybody's piece. A card, a hireling or a locked piece: nothing happens.
1
Take a warrior One warrior from your supply appears standing at your pointer, wherever it is. Yours is the faction you picked last. An empty supply does nothing.
2
Take a token or building Hold one second on a token or building to choose that kind. From then on every tap brings one of that kind from its supply to your pointer. The choice is yours alone and sticks. Nothing chosen: nothing happens.
3
Knave prisoner Hover any warrior: it lies flat, locks and gets a black outline. Press again to stand it up where it lay. Unlocking it by hand only takes the mark off and leaves it where it is.
4
Set the home Box-select pieces (enclaves, warriors, buildings) and hold one second: where they stand becomes their home. Numpad 0 sends them back there and numpad 2 takes that kind from there. Nothing selected: the hovered piece. Kept through a reload, forgotten by a new game.
no pad
No numpad? Bind the five named keys in Options → Game Keys: "Move back to supply/initial position", "Move a warrior from own supply to cursor", "Move any token to cursor; set type by holding numpad 2 for 2 seconds" (the hold is one second), "Set warrior as a knave prisoner", "Set home of selected pieces; hold numpad 4 for 1 second".

The box score the walnut sheet beside the map, spawned with it

It keeps score by itself and never writes in chat. You only touch it to correct something.

  • It reads the VP markers on the score track. Rows follow the seats, names follow who sits there, the map and deck are read off the table.
  • Each turn pass writes the finishing faction's score into the round's column. A point scored before your turn shows at once and is confirmed by your own turn.
  • It updates when a marker or card is dropped or a VP button is pressed, with a safety read every four seconds. A bumped marker is caught by that.
  • Dominance: settle a marker on a dominance card and the sheet records turn and suit and offers DOM WIN. A Vagabond or the Knaves get COALITION instead. A marker reaching 30 ends the game.
  • Knaves: the three captains are read from the cards in the captains board's slots, until START.
  • END TURN records the highlighted faction when the TTS turn order is not running.
  • EDIT corrects anything: click a cell to type a score, a column number to declare the round, a portrait for whose turn it is, ▲ to reorder, ▼ for the Eyrie leader, captains or vagabond character, plus MAP DECK UNPICKED and the game name. A typed number stands until the marker says otherwise.
  • EXPORT writes the game as JSON into the TTS Notebook (tab "BoxScore") and opens the Root Database box: paste a one-time token from the site to upload the game.
  • INFO is the full manual, on the sheet itself.

The VP panel one per faction, above its Crafted Improvements board

  • – and + move that faction's VP marker one step on the track. The box score follows.
  • DRAW 1 CARD deals a card from the draw pile to your hand. With the frogs out it reads DRAW CARD and DRAW POND draws from the pond.
  • Only the faction's owner can use it, and that is the faction you picked last. Anyone else gets a quiet line in chat. The Riverfolk's card goes to their public hand.

The draft what happens after 3-, 4- or 5-Player Draft

  • Seats and turn order are random, even for a lone tester. Every seat gets its colour, its hand and the matching turn-order card. The pool is one militant faction plus the others; the 3-player draft deals four faction cards to three seats.
  • Each seat gets a light selector board showing the drafted factions. The first click takes that faction: the board vanishes, the faction's pieces appear at that seat and the other boards refresh.
  • Your faction is the one you picked last. Numpad 1, numpad 2 and the VP panel all follow that pick, so switching faction moves your supply with you.
  • Knaves: four captains are dealt and three are kept. Their board, and the three captains the box score shows, follow the cards in its slots.

The turn panel the clock, spawned with the map next to the box score

  • ROUND is read from the box score, so the two can never disagree. The clock is the turn in progress.
  • START begins turn 1 at the first seat, shuffles the deck once and starts the game recorder. If the sheet already holds a game it asks first, in red: THIS RESETS BOXSCORE. Press again within four seconds.
  • DEAL 5 CARDS deals five from the shared deck to every seat. It asks before dealing a second time.
  • PAUSE stops the clock and becomes CONTINUE. The break is not counted.
  • Past twenty minutes the panel flashes red. Soft on purpose: no sound, nothing interrupted. It does not flash while paused. Three quick presses of DEAL show the flash for fifteen seconds.

The deck holder draw, discard, dominance and the pond

  • Drop a card on the discard: it turns face up and snaps onto the pile.
  • A dominance card dropped there flies to its own slot. A frog card goes back to the pond. Cards that shuffle on discard go back into the deck.
  • The frog pond is a drop spot too: a card dropped on it turns face up. A card that is not a frog's is sent on to the discard.
  • The draw pile is never touched by the holder. It only ever puts cards in.

Resync on the setup board, bottom row

  • Re-sends every object to every player. Press it when someone is missing pieces or sees card backs where the rest see faces. It destroys nothing.
  • It refuses to run while anyone holds a piece with the mouse and says who. Cards in your hand are fine. Drop what you hold and press again.
  • After a respawn it counts the cards. A duplicate lying loose is removed; a twin inside a deck or a hand is reported and left for a person to sort out.
  • Only you should see the difference. Seats, turn order and hands are put back exactly as they were.

The setup board things worth knowing

  • A button that wipes something asks twice. The first click turns it red and says what it would reset, the second within three seconds does it. With nothing to wipe there is no question.
  • A new game never touches the map, and a map button never touches the factions. The one exception is the Marsh, which is rebuilt when the four- and five-player boards no longer fit the table.
  • The box score and the turn panel stay put. They are never destroyed by a map change or a new game. Only START clears the sheet.
  • MORE swaps the two bottom rows for RIVERBOAT (the Flotilla hireling, wipes nothing) and CREDITS. BACK returns.
  • FACTION CARDS also lays out the twelve vagabond characters in a row behind the captains deck.
  • Knaves: drop a captain card into a slot of the captains board and its meeple, its two items and its warrior appear by themselves.
  • The snap points beside the suit markers take enclaves only. A relic or anything else passes them by.
  • CLEAR ALL empties the table but keeps the setup board, the hand zones and the table pieces. It asks like the rest.
Two rules the mod lives by. A piece is put where it belongs by the piece itself, never by who pressed the key, so being wrong about who you are costs nothing. And a key with nothing to do does nothing, silently.
