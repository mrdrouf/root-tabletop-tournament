Grid & units

Everything is measured in board units. The board is 207×170 units (X −90…117, Y −85…85, Y up). With snap on, pieces lock to the grid so rows and columns line up automatically. Arrow keys nudge by 1 unit (Shift = one grid step).

Notes

Faint text on the board (title, credit, logo) is baked into the wood texture — I can replace that whole texture separately. Editable text pieces are the plaques and the credit; game icons keep their art.
